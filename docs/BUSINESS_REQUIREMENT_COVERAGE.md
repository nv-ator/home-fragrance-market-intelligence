## 2. Complementary Unit Economics Insight

In addition to the 8 mandatory business areas above, the Business Insights page includes an explicit diagnostic for **Unit Economics & Packaging Metrics** (Card 6):
- **Observation**: Liquids average ₹1,203.48 / 100ml (n=231, median ₹665.00) vs. solids at ₹369.28 / 100g (n=23, median ₹330.00).
- **Interpretation**: Formulations in liquid format yield higher unit realization per standard volume than solid evaporative blocks.
- **Business Question**: Are higher unit realizations on liquid refills offset by packaging and dispensing hardware costs relative to solid formats?
- **Validation Required**: Review gross margin contribution by physical format, cost of goods sold (COGS) breakdowns, and device-to-refill attachment ratios.
